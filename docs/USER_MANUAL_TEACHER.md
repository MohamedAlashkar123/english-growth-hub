# Professional English Growth Hub - Teacher Manual

## 1. What is Professional English Growth Hub?

Professional English Growth Hub is a self-directed English learning platform for adult professionals. It helps learners organize their learning, track progress, and practice speaking, writing, listening, and pronunciation.

**Important:** This app is a learning tracker and organizer. It is not a complete English course. It does not teach new content. It does not give automatic feedback. It does not replace a teacher.

Think of it as a digital notebook and planner that helps your learner stay organized and motivated.

---

## 2. Who is this app for?

This app is for:
- Adult professionals (age 25+)
- Learners at B1+, B2, or C1 level
- Learners who work in business or technical environments
- Learners who want to improve professional English skills
- Learners who can study independently but need structure and tracking

This app is NOT for:
- Complete beginners (A1-A2)
- Children or teenagers
- Learners who need step-by-step instruction
- Learners who want automatic correction and feedback

---

## 3. What this app can help teachers do

As a teacher, you can use this app to:

- **See learner progress** - Track skills, levels, and improvement over time
- **Review practice** - See speaking topics, writing samples, and mistakes
- **Identify gaps** - Find weak areas that need more attention
- **Adjust plans** - Change intensity and focus based on learner needs
- **Give targeted feedback** - Use data to provide specific, helpful feedback
- **Save time** - All learner data is in one place
- **Motivate learners** - Show them their progress and achievements

---

## 4. What this app cannot do

This app cannot:
- Teach new grammar or vocabulary
- Correct learner mistakes automatically
- Give speaking or writing feedback
- Provide listening practice materials
- Replace teacher-student interaction
- Assess speaking or writing quality
- Generate new learning content automatically

**You are still the teacher.** This app helps you track and organize, but you must still teach, correct, and guide.

## 5. How to set up for your learner

### 5.1 Create learner profile

Ask your learner to open the app and go to **Profile**. Help them enter:
- Name
- Current level (B1+, B2, or C1)
- Target level
- Learning goal (e.g., "Speak more confidently in meetings")
- Available study time per day
- Days per week available for study

### 5.2 Choose plan intensity

Help your learner choose the right intensity:
- **Light** - 30-45 min/day, 12 months to target level
- **Standard** - 45-60 min/day, 9 months to target level
- **Intensive** - 60-90 min/day, 6 months to target level
- **Sprint** - 90-120 min/day, 3 months to target level

Choose based on learner's time availability and motivation.

### 5.3 Set initial skills assessment

Ask your learner to complete an initial evaluation in the **Evaluation** section. This gives you a baseline to track progress.

If you have already assessed the learner, you can enter the scores manually.

### 5.4 Create backup

Go to **Settings** and click **Export Data**. Save the file. This is your backup. Do this regularly.

### 5.5 Explain the app to your learner

Show your learner:
- How to open the app
- How to see the Daily Plan
- How to complete tasks
- How to add notes and mistakes
- How to export data for you to review

## 6. How to use the Dashboard

The Dashboard shows an overview of learner progress:

- **Current Level** - The learner's official level (you must approve changes)
- **Suggested Level** - What the app thinks the learner's level should be
- **Skill Scores** - Average scores for each of the 8 skills
- **Recent Activity** - Last few tasks completed
- **Weekly Progress** - Tasks completed this week

**Teacher Tip:** Check the Dashboard before each lesson. It gives you a quick snapshot of what your learner has been doing.

---

## 7. How to review Daily Plans

Ask your learner to share their Daily Plan with you. You can see:
- Tasks for the day
- Which tasks are completed
- Time spent on each task
- Notes added

**Teacher Tip:** Use Daily Plans to check if your learner is following the plan. If they are not completing tasks, ask why and adjust the plan if needed.

---

## 8. How to use the Roadmap

The Roadmap shows the learning journey over 9 months. Each month has:
- Focus areas (e.g., grammar, speaking, vocabulary)
- Key milestones
- Recommended activities

**Teacher Tip:** Use the Roadmap to plan your lessons. If Month 3 focuses on "Presentations," prepare presentation practice for that month.

- Business English
- Meeting English
- Technical English
- Smart Parks/Work-specific
- Vendor Communication
- Presentations
- Problem Solving
- Procurement
- Risk Management
- General Fluency

Each topic includes:
- Title and difficulty level
- Speaking structure (steps to follow)
- Useful phrases
- Prompt questions

**Teacher Tip:** Ask your learner to practice 2-3 speaking topics per week. In your lesson, ask follow-up questions and give feedback on their speaking.

- Rate 8 skills (Speaking, Listening, Grammar, Vocabulary, Pronunciation, Writing, Fluency, Confidence)

## 13. How to use Level & Assessment

## 19. How to track learner progress

## 20. How to give feedback

When giving feedback:
1. **Be specific** - Instead of "Good job," say "You used the past tense correctly in 3 out of 4 sentences."
2. **Focus on patterns** - Don't correct every mistake. Focus on repeated patterns.
3. **Be positive** - Start with what they did well, then mention areas to improve.
4. **Give action steps** - Tell them exactly what to practice next.
5. **Use app data** - Refer to evaluations, mistakes, and recommendations from the app.

**Example:** "Looking at your evaluation, your Grammar score improved from 3.2 to 4.1. Great progress on articles! However, I see you're still struggling with prepositions. Let's practice 5 preposition exercises this week."

---

## 21. How to adjust the learning plan

Adjust the plan when:
- Learner is not completing tasks (reduce intensity)
- Learner is bored or not challenged (increase intensity)
- Learner has specific needs (change focus areas)
- Learner has made significant progress (move to next level)

**To adjust:**
1. Go to Profile
2. Change plan intensity or focus areas
3. The app will update the Daily Plan automatically

**Teacher Tip:** Discuss changes with your learner first. Explain why the change is needed and get their agreement.

---

## 22. Common mistakes teachers make

1. **Treating the app as a complete course** - Remember, it's a tracker, not a teacher.
2. **Changing levels too often** - Wait for clear evidence before accepting a new level.
3. **Ignoring learner notes** - Notes show what the learner is thinking and struggling with.
4. **Not reviewing mistakes** - Mistakes are valuable learning opportunities.
5. **Focusing only on weaknesses** - Also build on strengths to keep motivation high.
6. **Not giving feedback** - The app tracks data, but you must give feedback.
7. **Adjusting the plan too much** - Give the plan time to work before changing it.

---

## 23. Best practices for faster learner improvement

1. **Check the app before each lesson** - See what your learner has been doing.
2. **Review mistakes regularly** - Focus on repeated patterns.
3. **Use speaking topics** - Ask your learner to practice and give feedback.
4. **Complete evaluations every 2-4 weeks** - Track progress and adjust plans.
5. **Give specific, actionable feedback** - Tell them exactly what to practice.
6. **Celebrate progress** - Show them their improvement using app data.
7. **Connect app practice to real life** - Use speaking topics that relate to their work.
8. **Be consistent** - Regular check-ins and feedback lead to better results.

---

## 24. Limitations and recommended next steps

### Limitations

- The app does not teach new content
- The app does not give automatic feedback
- The app does not replace a teacher
- The app requires self-discipline from the learner
- The app is local-only (no cloud sync or multi-device support)

### Recommended next steps

1. **Combine with other resources** - Use textbooks, videos, podcasts, and online courses.
2. **Schedule regular lessons** - Meet weekly or biweekly to review progress and give feedback.
3. **Set clear goals** - Help your learner set specific, measurable goals.
4. **Track long-term progress** - Use a spreadsheet or journal to track progress over months.
5. **Adjust based on results** - If something isn't working, change the approach.

---

**Final Note:** Professional English Growth Hub is a tool to help you and your learner stay organized and motivated. It is not a replacement for good teaching. Use it to track progress, identify gaps, and plan lessons, but remember: you are still the teacher.
