# Professional English Growth Hub - Quick Start Guide

## 1. Welcome to Professional English Growth Hub

This app helps you improve your professional English through organized daily practice, progress tracking, and structured learning plans.

**What you need to know:**
- This is a self-directed learning tool
- It runs locally in your browser
- Your data is stored on your device
- You should export regular backups

---

## 2. First-Time Setup (5 minutes)

### Step 1: Open the app
Open the app in your browser. You will see the main dashboard.

### Step 2: Complete your profile
Go to **Profile** and enter:
- Your name
- Current English level (B1+, B2, or C1)
- Target level
- Your learning goal
- Available study time per day
- Days per week you can study

### Step 3: Choose your plan intensity
Select one of these options:
- **Light** - 30-45 min/day, 12 months
- **Standard** - 45-60 min/day, 9 months
- **Intensive** - 60-90 min/day, 6 months
- **Sprint** - 90-120 min/day, 3 months

### Step 4: Complete your first evaluation
Go to **Evaluation** and rate yourself on the 8 skills. This gives you a starting point.

### Step 5: Create a backup
Go to **Settings** and click **Export Data**. Save the file somewhere safe.

---

## 3. Your Daily Routine (15-20 minutes)

Every day, follow this simple routine:

### 1. Check your Daily Plan
- Go to **Daily Plan**
- See what tasks are scheduled for today
- Note the time required for each task

### 2. Complete your tasks
- Click on each task to mark it complete
- Add notes about what you learned
- Record any mistakes you made

### 3. Practice speaking
- Go to **Speaking Topics**
- Choose a topic for today
- Follow the speaking structure
- Record your notes and mistakes

### 4. Review your progress
- Check the **Dashboard** to see your progress
- Look at your skill scores
- See what you've accomplished this week

---

## 4. Key Features Overview

### Dashboard
Shows your current level, skill scores, and recent activity. Check this daily.

### Daily Plan
Your personalized learning schedule for each day. Complete tasks to stay on track.

### Roadmap
Shows your 9-month learning journey with monthly focus areas and milestones.

### Speaking Topics
100 speaking topics organized by category. Practice 2-3 per week.

### Mistakes
Track grammar, vocabulary, and pronunciation mistakes. Review regularly to improve.

### Evaluation
Rate your skills every 2-4 weeks to track progress.

### Level & Assessment
See your official level and suggested level. Accept level changes when you're ready.

### Recommendations
Get personalized suggestions based on your progress.

### Content Library
Reference materials including grammar topics, business phrases, and common mistakes.

### Notes
Add notes about your learning journey.

### Export/Import
Backup your data and restore it if needed.

## 5. How to Use Speaking Topics

1. Go to **Speaking Topics**
2. Filter by category or difficulty if needed
3. Click on a topic to see details
4. Read the speaking structure (steps to follow)
5. Review the useful phrases
6. Think about the prompt questions
7. Practice speaking for the recommended time
8. Add notes about your performance
9. Record any mistakes you made

**Tip:** Practice speaking out loud. Record yourself if possible.

---

## 6. How to Track Mistakes

1. After any practice session, go to **Mistakes**
2. Click "Add Mistake"
3. Choose the type (Grammar, Vocabulary, or Pronunciation)
4. Enter the mistake and the correction
5. Add a note about why you made this mistake
6. Save

**Tip:** Review your mistakes weekly. Focus on the ones you repeat often.

---

## 7. How to Complete Evaluations

1. Go to **Evaluation**
2. Rate yourself on each of the 8 skills (1-5 scale)
3. Add comments for each skill if you want
4. Save the evaluation
5. Compare with previous evaluations to see progress

**Tip:** Complete an evaluation every 2-4 weeks. Don't do it too often.

---

## 8. How to Backup Your Data

**Important:** Your data is stored locally. If you clear your browser data or change devices, you will lose everything unless you have a backup.

### To backup:
1. Go to **Settings**
2. Click "Export Data"
3. Save the JSON file to your computer or cloud storage

### To restore:
1. Go to **Settings**
2. Click "Import Data"
3. Select your backup file
4. Confirm the import

**Tip:** Backup weekly or after important evaluations.

## 9. Tips for Success

### Be consistent
Study at the same time every day. Consistency is more important than intensity.

### Start small
If you're new to self-directed learning, start with the Light or Standard intensity.

### Focus on speaking
Speaking is often the most important skill for professionals. Practice speaking topics regularly.

### Review your mistakes
Mistakes are your best teachers. Review them often and create practice exercises.

### Don't change levels too often
Wait for clear evidence of improvement before accepting a new level.

### Use other resources
This app is a tracker, not a complete course. Use textbooks, videos, podcasts, and other resources to learn new content.

### Get feedback
If possible, work with a teacher or language partner who can give you feedback.

### Be patient
Language learning takes time. Celebrate small wins and keep going.

## 10. What to Do If You Get Stuck

### Problem: I don't understand a task
**Solution:** Skip it for now and move on. Ask a teacher or look up the topic online.

### Problem: I'm not making progress
**Solution:** 
- Check if you're completing tasks consistently
- Review your mistakes and focus on repeated patterns
- Consider adjusting your plan intensity
- Get feedback from a teacher

### Problem: The app is not working
**Solution:**
- Try refreshing the page
- Clear your browser cache and reload
- Import your backup data
- Check that you're using a supported browser

### Problem: I lost my data
**Solution:** 
- If you have a backup, import it
- If you don't have a backup, you will need to start over
- This is why regular backups are important

### Problem: I don't know what level I am
**Solution:**
- Take a CEFR placement test online
- Ask a teacher to assess you
- Start with B1+ if you're unsure (you can adjust later)
