## 11. Content Quality Standards

### Writing Standards
- Clear, professional English
- Appropriate difficulty for target level
- Realistic business contexts
- Culturally appropriate
- Free of errors

### Audio Standards
- Clear pronunciation (native or near-native)
- Appropriate speed (not too fast, not too slow)
- Good audio quality
- Consistent accent (choose one primary accent)

### Review Process
1. Content creation by subject matter expert
2. Review by English language teacher
3. Level verification by CEFR specialist
4. Technical review for app integration
5. User testing (if possible)

## Conclusion

This content expansion strategy will transform Professional English Growth Hub from a basic tracking tool into a comprehensive learning platform. By adding listening, reading, writing, pronunciation, and vocabulary content, we can provide a more complete learning experience while maintaining the app's focus on self-directed, professional English improvement.

The phased approach allows for gradual implementation and continuous feedback, ensuring that new content meets user needs and quality standards.
